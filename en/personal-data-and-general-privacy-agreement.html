Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Personal Data and General Privacy Agreement applicable to our mobile applications, websites, and streaming services of RadioNube and ALTOMARKETING LLC.

Policy Scope

This policy governs the processing of information and privacy across applications for Android (Google Play), iOS (App Store), web players, and services provided by RadioNube.online and the product network of ALTOMARKETING LLC.

1

Privacy Commitment and Data Collection

This privacy policy includes all details regarding data collected in our applications and services, and the manner in which it is used. As users ourselves, we do not like sharing personal information without knowing precisely how that data will be processed. Our applications thrive thanks to the trust of our users, and we are committed to safeguarding that trust at all times.

Anonymous Data: The application collects strictly anonymous usage data. This information does not include original device serial identifiers (IMEI / Serial), your real identity, nor your private personal data, unless directly provided by you in an express form.
Seguridad y Compras: La aplicación no puede acceder a su información sensible real de compras o pagos sin asociarla expresamente a sus datos de cliente.
2

Registration Forms and Purpose

En ciertas aplicaciones se recomienda completar el formulario de registro voluntario. Los datos ingresados tienen como único propósito:

Permitir al equipo técnico contactar al usuario de manera rápida y eficiente para brindar soporte.
Resolver eventuales incidencias de licenciamiento o activación de funciones especiales.
Facilitar la recuperación de servicios o preferencias personalizadas.
3

Application Usage and Interactions

Las estadísticas de uso y las interacciones dentro de la aplicación se utilizan para determinar qué emisoras o contenidos son los más escuchados y qué funciones despiertan mayor interés, lo que nos permite optimizar y actualizar constantemente la plataforma.

Asimismo, estos datos permiten listar las emisoras más populares según regiones específicas o proveer métricas agregadas a los administradores de cada estación. Ninguno de estos servicios incluye información sensible o datos de identificación personal sin su autorización previa y explícita.

4

Communication with Third-Party Servers

En determinados casos, la aplicación se comunica directamente con servidores de terceros para obtener datos actualizados en tiempo real (por ejemplo: fuentes RSS de noticias, imágenes de canciones/artistas y metadatos de audio emitido).

Cuando esto ocurre, no transmitimos datos sobre usted ni sobre sus patrones de navegación a dichos terceros, salvo en los casos en que se señale expresamente. Le sugerimos revisar las políticas de privacidad de los proveedores externos cuando corresponda.

5

Advertising and Sponsored Banners

La aplicación puede mostrar anuncios publicitarios o banners patrocinados en distintas secciones. Este sistema puede emplear información básica no sensible de idioma o país para ofrecer contenido comercialmente relevante.

En ciertas situaciones comerciales o versiones personalizadas (marca blanca o planes prémium), la publicidad puede estar deshabilitada o sustituida por patrocinios propios de la emisora.

6

Third-Party Content and Images

La aplicación puede desplegar páginas web en vivo, widgets o imágenes provistas por fuentes de terceros. En estos supuestos, resultan aplicables los términos y políticas fijados por dichos sitios web. Utilizamos servicios como Google Image Search para asociar carátulas y nombres de temas a las transmisiones en vivo (Now Playing).

7

Official Privacy Policy (English Version for App Stores)

This privacy policy includes all the details about the data collected in this app and how it’s used. As a user we also do not like to give personal informations and we don’t want to see our informations collected without knowing what will going to happen to those datas. This app has been alive with the support and trust of it’s users. We want to keep the trust; you can find all the details about the data usage below.

Anonymous Data: This app does collect anonymous usage data. This data does not include your original device identification numbers, your real personality or your personal data if it’s not directly given by you in an open form. This App can not access to your real information and your purchases or any other action may not be restored without associating purchases with some information. This is why it is recommended to fill the registration form in the application. That form contains personal information which helps us contact the user easily, help quickly and solve possible licensing problems.

Usage / Interactions: Usage and interactions in the application are used to determine what stations is being listed at most and which functions attracts the most attention so we can improve those sections. This information also allows us to be able to serve better features like listing most popular stations according to a specific region. These datas may be used to serve additional services to 3rd parties like station statistics. NONE of these services will include sensitive information/personally identifying data if not permitted by you.

Third-Party Communication: In some cases this app may communicate directly with a 3rd party server to obtain latest data for display within the app (such as rss feeds, artist/song images and informations). When this happens – we don’t transmit any data about you or your usage to these 3rd parties except where explicitly stated. Please check these 3rd parties (where applicable) for their additional privacy policies.

Advertising Banner Privacy Policy: This app may display advertisements on various places in the application. This system may use and transmit basic regional/language information about you to the advertising banner system in order to provide you with relevant ads. In some cases, we may have agreements with sponsors and we may hide advertisements in that situation. Eventhough the advertisement may get hidden in that situation, we may provide similar informations to sponsors or 3rd parties.

3rd Party Content Privacy Policy: This app may display live web pages or images from 3rd party sources and in these cases you should read the privacy policies displayed by these websites. We use Google Image Search for some of the images related to now playing info.
