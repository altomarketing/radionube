To start your online radio, all you need is a PC or laptop with an internet connection of 1 Mbps or higher. We provide an easy-to-use broadcasting software that you simply launch to start streaming live.
Any audio playing on your PC will be sent to the streaming server—so you can play music with ZaraRadio, RadioBOSS, host live calls on Zoom or Skype, or play YouTube clips. In short, all sound on your computer streams directly to your audience.
Optionally, you can connect an external mixing console or microphones, but that is not required to start.

There are 3 ways to broadcast: View complete tutorial here

  1. Live Only: Everything streams directly from your PC. When you are on air, listeners hear you; when you stop, the stream goes silent.

  2. AutoDJ Only (Cloud MP3s): Runs 100% in the cloud without keeping your PC on. Upload MP3s to your panel, add jingles, liners, and schedule playlists. Plays 24/7 automatically.

  3. AutoDJ + Live Hybrid: Go live anytime, and the Cloud AutoDJ kicks in automatically the moment you finish.

Your PC sends your music and voiceover to our 📡 Radio Server 📡, and from there listeners can tune in across multiple platforms SIMULTANEOUSLY.

  • We provide a dedicated web page for your listeners, featuring player, photos, and station info

  • We provide embeddable HTML5 players for your existing website.

  • We generate direct links to share via WhatsApp, social media, or email

  • We offer mobile apps so your listeners can take your radio wherever they go
